Common Membership Questions

Can we pause or cancel our membership anytime?
+
Yes, absolutely! There are no long-term contracts. You can pause during family vacations or cancel anytime with a single click in your parent portal.
Do you offer sibling discounts?
+
Yes! We offer a 15% discount for second and third siblings enrolled in any of our monthly programs. The discount is applied automatically upon checkout.
What payment methods do you accept?
+
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and automatic monthly bank debits.
