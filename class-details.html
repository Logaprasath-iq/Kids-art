Acrylic Painting Track

Creative Acrylic & Canvas Studio

A vibrant, joyful course where budding young artists discover the excitement of mixing vivid colors, applying expressive brushstrokes, and building gallery-ready stretched canvases.

Ages 5–8 60 Min Weekly Max 8 Kids Supplies Included
Enroll in Class — $45/session Book Free Trial

What Kids Learn

This course is designed around playful sensory exploration rather than rigid repetition. Every student works on real wooden-framed canvases with artist-grade, non-toxic heavy body acrylics.

  • 01 Color Wheel & Harmony: Understanding primary, secondary, and complementary tones through direct experiential mixing.
  • 02 Brush Handling & Mark Making: Flat brushes, filberts, fan brushes, and sponge dabbing to create expressive textures.
  • 03 Layering & Opacity: Background washes, midground horizons, and crisp foreground details.
  • 04 Artistic Voice: Choosing personal color palettes and titling their artwork for studio exhibitions.

4-Week Curriculum

Week 1: The Magic of Primary Pigments

Color theory games, mixing rainbow palettes, and creating an expressive sunset gradient.

Week 2: Fantastic Creatures & Texture

Using palette knives and sponges to sculpt the scales, fur, and feathers of wild creatures.

Week 3: Nature & Impressionist Dabs

Inspired by Claude Monet's water lilies; building atmospheric lighting and water reflections.

Week 4: Final Masterpiece & Gallery Varnishing

Completing a 12x16 inch stretched canvas, adding a protective gloss coat, and signing like a master!

Got Questions?

Class Frequently Asked Questions

What if my child has never painted on canvas before?
+
No prior experience is necessary! Our mentors scaffold every exercise step-by-step with warm encouragement. Most of our enrolled students started with zero formal background.
Are paints and protective aprons provided?
+
Yes, 100% of supplies—including kid-sized canvas boards, stretched gallery canvases, non-toxic water-based acrylics, aprons, and brush sets—are fully included in tuition.
What is your missed class / makeup policy?
+
We understand family schedules change. Simply notify us 24 hours prior and you can book a free makeup session during any of our open Saturday maker studios.
Can parents stay and watch the session?
+
Parents are welcome to relax in our parent lounge with complimentary coffee and Wi-Fi, which overlooks the studio through observation windows.
